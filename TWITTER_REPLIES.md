# Twitter Replies

Manual tracking and rules for Twitter/X reply triage.

## Reply Priority

### High Priority
- Direct questions about Triger.sh
- Bug reports
- Security-related mentions

### Medium Priority
- Feature requests
- Documentation improvement suggestions
- High-engagement general mentions

### Low Priority
- General mentions
- Retweets / quotes
